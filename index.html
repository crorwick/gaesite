Global Alliance Events is a two-person partnership. Chris Rorwick runs transportation logistics. Cindy Rorwick runs event production. When you hire us, those are the people who show up.

Chris has spent twenty-five years moving executives, clients and VIPs through events where the schedule cannot slip — the Masters, the Super Bowl, Cannes Lions, Pebble Beach, the FIFA World Cup. Cindy has spent a decade producing large-scale technology and medical device programs, most recently Meta Connect and the Horizons Creator Summit.

We work directly with corporate clients and as a vendor partner to event agencies. Our piece arrives fully staffed and self-managed, which means one accountable contact rather than a stack of vendors pointing at each other.
